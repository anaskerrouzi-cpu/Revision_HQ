GCSE revision toolkit

🧠 Active recall

Close the book and write or say everything you remember. Then check gaps.

🗂️ Flashcards

One question per card. Revisit difficult cards more often.

✍️ Exam questions

Practise under timed conditions, mark with a mark scheme, then improve the answer.

🔁 Spaced review

Revisit topics tomorrow, later in the week, and again next week.

📖 English

Plan paragraphs, choose short quotations and explain how evidence supports your point.

➗ Maths & Science

Show working, learn equations and units, and retry questions you got wrong.

🌍 History & Geography

Test dates, case-study facts, causes and consequences; practise command words.

💼 Business

Learn key terms and apply them to the business in the question. Explain the impact.
